Churn calls

auto-refreshes every 15s

A user stops using your product. Meoww phones them, asks why, and turns that conversation into a record someone can act on.

1 A churn signal arrives: a bad review, a cancelled plan, an account gone quiet.
2 The voice agent calls and interviews them, following up on whatever they say.
3 The call becomes a typed record: reason, severity, the price they would have stayed at.
4 Each field is scored, and marked as either stated by the user or inferred.

The last step is the point. Anyone can summarise a call. Knowing which parts of that summary the user actually said, and which the model guessed, is what makes the data safe to act on.
